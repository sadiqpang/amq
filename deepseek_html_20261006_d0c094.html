About the Madrasa

A tradition of light, a future of purpose

Al Madrasatul Qadiriya was founded in the year 1999 in the quiet village of Pang, Malappuram, by a small group of scholars and well-wishers who dreamed of a place where the Qur'an would be taught with excellence and where children would grow into upright, useful members of society.

What began in a single room with eleven students has, by the grace of Allah, grown into a thriving institution serving more than two hundred and sixty students across foundation, intermediate, advanced and Hifz streams. Our teachers are graduates of renowned Islamic institutions, and our doors remain open to every child of the community.

We believe knowledge and character are inseparable. Alongside Qur'an, Arabic and the Islamic sciences, our students are formed in adab, discipline and service — preparing them to carry the light of faith into whatever path they choose.

Curriculum

The Syllabus

A carefully graded course of study spanning ten years — from the first Arabic letter to the advanced Islamic sciences.

Ihthidai
ابتدائي

The foundational stage, where children take their first steps into the Qur'an and the Arabic language. Learning here is gentle, joyful and rooted in love — with stories, songs and plenty of encouragement.

  • Grades1 – 3
  • Duration3 Years
  • Class Hours7:00 – 10:30 AM
  • AssessmentMonthly + Annual
Subjects Offered
Qur'an Recitation Nazira with correct pronunciation
Tajweed Basics Makharij and simple rules
Arabic Alphabet Reading, writing and joining
Du'as & Adhkar Daily and occasional supplications
Seerah Stories Life of the Prophet ﷺ for children
Akhlaq Manners, honesty, respect
Malayalam Reading and writing
Islamic Basics Pillars, faith and prayer
Sanavi
ثانوي

The intermediate stage builds depth. Students move from reciting to understanding, from memorising to reasoning — studying the Qur'an with meaning, Islamic law, and the structure of the Arabic language.

  • Grades4 – 7
  • Duration4 Years
  • Class Hours7:00 – 12:00 PM
  • AssessmentTerm-wise + Annual
Subjects Offered
Qur'an with Meaning Word-by-word translation
Fiqh Worship, transactions, daily rulings
Aqeedah Foundations of belief
Hadith Basics Selected narrations with explanation
Nahw & Sarf Arabic grammar and morphology
Islamic History From the Prophet ﷺ to the present
Urdu Reading, writing and comprehension
Malayalam & English Language and expression
Aali
عالي

The advanced stage prepares scholars. Students engage directly with classical texts, learn the tools of independent reasoning, and are trained to teach, lead and serve the community with confidence.

  • Grades8 – 10
  • Duration3 Years
  • Class Hours7:00 – 1:00 PM
  • AssessmentSemester + Final + Thesis
Subjects Offered
Tafsir Classical and contemporary commentary
Hadith Sciences Mustalah and selected texts
Usul al-Fiqh Principles of Islamic jurisprudence
Balagha Arabic rhetoric and eloquence
Arabic Literature Poetry and classical prose
Mantiq Logic and sound reasoning
Comparative Religion Dialogue and understanding
Teaching Practice Classroom training and delivery
Hifz ul Qur'an
حفظ القرآن

A dedicated, residential-style memorisation programme for students who show aptitude and commitment. Guided by experienced huffaz, with daily revision, tajweed refinement and structured targets.

  • Duration3 – 5 Years
  • Daily Portion1 – 1.5 Pages
  • Class Hours5:00 AM – 1:00 PM
  • AssessmentWeekly + Monthly Revision
Programme Components
New Lesson Daily fresh memorisation
Sabq Revision Recent portion review
Manzil Revision Long-term retention cycle
Tajweed Refinement Pronunciation correction
Qira'at Training Melodious recitation practice
Tafsir Sessions Meaning of memorised verses
Adhkar & Du'a Daily spiritual routine
Dastar Bandi Graduation ceremony upon completion
Occasions & Gatherings

Events at the Madrasa

Our calendar is woven with the rhythms of the Islamic year — seasons of worship, remembrance, celebration and community.

Rabi' al-Awwal

Milad un-Nabi ﷺ

Our largest annual gathering — a week of qasida recitation, scholarly talks on the life of the Prophet ﷺ, a candlelit procession through Pang, and a community feast for all.

Annual · 7 Days
Ramadan

Iftar & Taraweeh

Daily iftar served to students and the community, special taraweeh prayers led by our senior huffaz, and a full Qur'an completion (Khatm) on the 27th night.

30 Days · Daily
Sha'ban

Annual Uroos

A three-day spiritual celebration honouring our founding scholars — with dhikr circles, speeches, Khatm ul Qur'an and a grand public gathering on the final evening.

3 Days · Community
End of Academic Year

Dastar Bandi — Hifz Graduation

The most cherished day in our calendar — honouring students who have completed the memorisation of the entire Qur'an, with the traditional turban ceremony and du'a.

Annual · Ceremony
Rajab

Qira'at & Islamic Quiz Competition

An inter-madrasa competition drawing participants from across Malappuram — categories in Qur'an recitation, tajweed, Islamic knowledge and Arabic speech.

Regional · 2 Days
Shawwal & Dhul Hijjah

Eid Milap & Qurbani

Eid prayer gatherings on our grounds, a shared breakfast for the community, and the annual Qurbani distribution reaching families across the locality.

Twice Yearly
